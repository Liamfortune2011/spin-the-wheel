Sevens

Your hand

The whole deck is dealt out to four players. The player holding the 7 of diamonds starts with it. After that, anyone can play a 7 to open its suit, and every other card must sit next to one already on the table, so a 6 or an 8 goes beside a 7, a 5 beside a 6, and so on up to the kings and down to the aces. If you have no card that fits, you pass. The first player to empty their hand wins.
