Twenty-One

Dealer

You

Your bet
0

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count 1 or 11, face cards count 10. The dealer draws to 16 and stands on every 17. Blackjack pays 3 to 2. You can double your bet on your first two cards for one more card. Bets are 10 and up, and the game uses a four-deck shoe. Your chips are saved on this device.
