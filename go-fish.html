Go Fish

    Your hand

    Everyone starts with five cards. On your turn, click a card in your hand to pick a rank, click the player you want to ask, then press Ask. If they hold that rank they hand over every card of it and you go again. If not, they say go fish and you draw from the pile. Drawing the rank you asked for also lets you go again. Four of a kind makes a book. When all 13 books are made, the player with the most books wins.
